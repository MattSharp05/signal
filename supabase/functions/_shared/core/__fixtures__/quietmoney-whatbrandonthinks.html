Where the numbers in this chart come from

The main engine is branded output: the roast reports Brandon writes are made to be easy to screenshot and send back to the group chat, and people named in the group get curious and upload their own groups too.

The turbo boost is a self-built UGC creator team: GumGum Creators pays a base fee per video and a bonus when one blows up, and posts reaction videos of people watching Brandon roast group chats in bulk on TikTok and Instagram.

By estimate (visits are estimated), about 40% of visits are brought by group members' screenshots; roughly 29 of every 100 visits click through to the checkout page.

Model: Lenny's racing growth framework. Engine = branded output (the report is made easy to screenshot back into the group chat, and group members who are named get curious and also come to upload); turbo boost = a self-built UGC creator team posting reaction videos in bulk; the free preview of the first report lowers the paywall to the minimum.
