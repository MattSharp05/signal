The cool origin story

We started coding and shipping products on the side of our studies. Nights, weekends, the back of the classroom. At some point the side thing became the main thing, and we finally dropped out.

What were we building? Always products for people, never for companies. For us, building a product means crafting an experience. Crafting a magical moment. Making you feel an emotion, feel the wow! That is why we only work in consumer.

And why coaching and self-help? Because we didn't go for the quick buck. We went for what we love. We are passionate about philosophy, psychology, everything that makes a human being a little better. We're inspired by so many people: Tony Robbins, Naval, The School of Life, Mark Manson, and so many more. And as someone smart said: if it feels like play to you, you'll outcompete everyone who is working. So we put our two passions together: building products, and upgrading humans.

Matthias had already been building in this space for a while, with one deep conviction: AI can change this game. It can improve us as humans. Emotionally. Our software, our way of thinking, our patterns. I joined him after exiting my first company.

It's a bit blurry to explain the products and apps we were making back then. We were exploring. But the effect we kept finding was this: just by talking with a well-made AI chatbot, one with personality, made with taste and craft, you can get to a wow moment pretty fast. It's like talking to a philosopher, or a Tony Robbins kind of guy, who knows you kind of well. He helps you get clarity. He helps you figure out what you want to do with your life, the direction it's going, what you need to do to get there. We were building tons of experiences like that: helping you find your purpose, and work toward it.

How Brandon was born

Let's be honest: we failed. A lot. We shipped a lot of apps and products in coaching and self-help. AI coaches. Journals. Programs. We were sure every time. Almost nobody used them.

It hurt. But it was our school. It taught us how to write prompts, how to build a character, how to build depth, how to build a real analysis instead of polite, empty advice. And it taught us what we are against: AI that flatters you. Most AI tells you what you want to hear. We work actively in the opposite direction.

And every time, our problem was the same: the cold start. To make a self-help report that really hits, you had to answer around 50 questions about your life. Doing it well took hours. Nobody does that.

Meanwhile, I was on a side quest, while Matthias kept iterating on the app we had on the App Store. My thing was simple: you export a group chat, and an AI roasts it. We thought it was a completely different product from everything we had built before. Nothing related. Something light and viral.

We tried it on our own family and friend group chats. And it was insane. People were crying laughing, sending the report to the group, and then three more people in the group tried it with their own chats. We did nothing. No explaining, no convincing. People used it, shared it, and it spread by itself. After years of products nobody wanted, we hit product-market fit instantly.

And we still didn't connect the dots. This light, viral thing was actually solving the cold start problem of our apps. You export a chat in less than two minutes, and the AI gets a hundred times the context it needs to write something incredible. No 50 questions. Years of you and the people in your life, already written down. A game changer, and we were looking right past it.

When Matthias realised it, we merged what we know. Me, I'm more about virality, fun, light. Matthias is about depth, psychology, character. Mixing the two gave birth to Brandon. He can roast the fuck out of your friend group, and he can also go deep on what he sees in the way you act with your girlfriend: the thing you could improve, the thing that shows up across all your relationships.

If you use Brandon and you are reading this, now you see it: all our previous work in self-help, all those AI chatbots with a personality, it is all inside Brandon. He looks viral when you see him on TikTok. When you use him, you understand the depth we put into building this character.

And why the name Brandon? It's pretty funny. Our previous AI was called Leo. Giving an AI a name and a personality is how you create a brand, a connection, a writing style that feels human and hits you emotionally. So we brainstormed names. When I was young, I told my friends that my rapper beatmaker name was Brandon. Just like that, it became my weird nickname in the friend group. Robin, a.k.a. Brandon. Weird, but pretty funny. And because Brandon started as a group chat product, I thought it was perfect.

And it is perfect! It's unique. Everybody will know Brandon. We're confident. We're ambitious. We work hard.

Brandon is Brandon

What does Brandon do? Why should you use it? Honestly, it is always so difficult for us to explain it to our family and friends. We usually just push people to try.

Here is my best try. You hand over one of the most intimate parts of your life, your messages. And you watch a character, someone with a real personality, read your whole chat ultra fast and comment out loud. "Oh yeah, you were funny there." "When you said this, I think the other person felt like that." "Wow, this line is crazy! I read chats every day and I never saw a line this hard." He just gives his opinion. And for some magical reason, it's amazing.

If I had to name one core thing, as a founder, it is this: Brandon helps you realise how you come across. How the people in your life might see you. The character you are building without noticing. Whether you bring tension into the room when you walk in, or joy.

Why does it work? Because nobody in your life can do this for you. Your friends are too kind. Your family is too involved. And nobody has read everything. Brandon has, and he has nothing to gain. So he can show you the role you play, next to the role you think you play. He can say the thing you both know and neither of you has said out loud. He can tell you what you are doing right that you are too close to see, and what you are doing wrong that nobody loves you enough to tell you.

And he does it with a heart. He judges the pattern, never the person. You should feel seen, not diagnosed. You should finish a report hopeful, not flattered. Honestly, even for us it is hard to put the magic into words. He is just being him, and saying what he thinks.

Brandon is not your friend, and he doesn't take your side in the argument. But he is rooting for you. Brandon is Brandon. He has his own personality, his own opinions, his own view. He was built, conceived and iterated over thousands of hours of reading and building in psychology and self-help, and over thousands and thousands of reports, with the feedback you send us. He is our moat. The copycats can copy the idea. They can't copy him.

People ask if he is the funny product or the deep product. He is just Brandon. Give him a friend group that mostly says stupid things, and people cry laughing. Give him a chat where people talk about real things, and he writes something deep, a really beautiful analysis of everyone in it. It's not two products. It's him, every time. What he says depends on the chat.

And there is so much more in your chat than you think. People always tell us: "There's nothing in there, we barely talk, we talk on another app, we talk in real life." No, no, no. Brandon makes magic with a family group where "nobody talks". Mum ends up crying laughing. It's incredible to watch.

One warning: there are many copycats out there. Please always use this website, whatbrandonthinks.com. Any other site or app that looks like Brandon is not us.

What Brandon does to people

We get tons of messages from people telling us how the product hit them. What we are sure about: if you read a Brandon report, you feel something. Whatever it is, you feel it.

The first big reaction is laughter. Really, hilarious. When we have dinner with friends, with family, with friends of family, we always push them to try. For thirty minutes, people are laughing everywhere.

The second is depth. Brandon is able to put words on things you kind of knew about yourself, about your relationship, about how you come across. And the way he writes it has a personality. It hits. It makes you think.

That one happened to me. Running my own conversations through Brandon changed me as a human. I saw how I come across, the role I play, and how hard it is for me to be vulnerable. The feeling is strange: a random AI dude puts into words things I knew but had never been able to express this clearly. Feelings and intuitions I had, written down black on white. And once it is written, once you can read it again and again, it changes everything. He knows how to make it land.

The third reaction is darker, at least it looks like it. So many couples break up after Brandon. Our gut feeling is around a hundred a day. We can't measure it, it's really a guess. People 20 to 30 years old, but also people ending a long marriage. It may sound like a bad thing to be proud of. We don't agree, and the people who write to us don't either. A lot of couples are together out of routine. They never stop, take a step back and ask where it is going. Many read the report together and say: okay, let's end this. They were losing their time. Our mission is to help you improve your relationships, and sometimes that means ending one.

And then the last one: a mum and her daughter, brothers, a whole family. They read it together, have a deep conversation, hug, cry, and change the way they are with each other. That is the self-help side of this product. It's there because of where we come from.

Where this is going

Once you are healthy, have something to eat and a place to sleep, what is the next thing a human needs? Other humans. Healthy relationships. We are social animals. This is the core of us.

Some people build products to replace a part of human relationships with AI. Fair to them. That is not what we do. The drive to see someone smile, to touch and hug another human, to have fun, to have dinner together: this is us. This is what being human is all about. Having good relationships with your family, your friends, the one you love.

So how do you get better at this game? How do you become aware of how you come across? Of how you turn bitter, toxic, passive-aggressive? Of why you struggle to open up and be vulnerable? Sometimes someone gives you honest feedback. Sometimes you realise it by yourself. It is rare, and it is slow.

Brandon makes that a hundred times faster. He is the intelligence layer on top of your relationships: he has the context on you, on the people in your life, and on how you really are with each other, because it is all in your messages.

And if AI can improve your relationships by only 20%, this is huge. It would change your life. Literally.

The need to get better at relationships is truer than ever. We strongly believe AI can help with that, every day, and Brandon is how we do it. Today it is love, friends and family. Tomorrow it is every relationship that matters in your life.

And we are not doing this small. Matthias and I now really have the ambition to build Brandon into a big company. A very ambitious one.

One last thing. Today, Brandon is a revelation business: you come, you read, it hits you. We are slowly moving to a habit business: Brandon in your life all year, chat after chat, as your relationships move. Our first tests and iterations of that are with the Brandon Membership. It is early, and we are still figuring it out.

Privacy is the whole company

You are handing us some of the most personal things you own. We know it. And having so many people trust us with something this personal really touches us.

So let's be clear about where our interest is. Selling your data, or doing anything with it other than writing your report, is THE OPPOSITE of our interest. What we are building is a brand you can trust. The day you stop trusting Brandon with your chat, there is no company left. We make money when you buy a report. That is the whole business.

And like the good cliché of European founders living in Paris that we are, your data is under GDPR, one of the strictest data protection laws in the world. It is not us being nice. It is the law here, and you can hold us to it.

It is extremely important for us to do everything in our power to make this smooth, secure and safe. Your chat is deleted automatically. It is never used to train AI. It is encrypted, on the way to us and once it is with us. I won't go through all the mechanics here. Click below if you want every detail.

Privacy isn't a feature here. It's not a nice thing to do. It's the whole company.
